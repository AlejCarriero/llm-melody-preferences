About the experiment

A language model was shown hundreds of pairs of short melodies and asked, each time, which one it preferred. Here you do the same task. At the end you see how consistent your own choices were, and how your taste compares with the model's.

What this can and can't tell you

One session of 20 or 30 choices is a small sample, so a single difference from the model means little. The model saw every melody only as text, and never heard any of them. If you chose to read the melodies, you had the same view it had; if you listened, you had something it never has.
